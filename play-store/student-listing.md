# OpenDojos Student listing draft

**App name:** OpenDojos Student

**Short description:** View your dojo profile, attendance, fees, progress, achievements and updates.

**Full description:**

OpenDojos Student gives enrolled students a secure view of information shared by their dojo. Students can review their profile, attendance, fee and receipt history, belt syllabus progress, grading information, achievements, notifications and downloadable reports where enabled.

Portal access is issued and managed by a participating dojo. An active internet connection and valid student portal credentials are required. The information available depends on the dojo's configuration.
