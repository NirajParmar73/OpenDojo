# OpenDojos Admin listing draft

**App name:** OpenDojos Admin

**Short description:** Manage students, attendance, fees, gradings and dojo operations in one place.

**Full description:**

OpenDojos Admin helps martial arts organizations manage day-to-day dojo operations. Authorized owners and staff can manage student records, attendance, fee tracking, receipts, belt syllabus progress, grading exams, locations, staff responsibilities and operational reports.

Access is provided by your OpenDojos organization. Available features depend on your role and organization plan.

Key capabilities include:

- Student enrollment and records
- Attendance and fee management
- Receipts and finance reporting
- Belt syllabus and grading workflows
- Multi-location staff access controls
- Student portal account administration

An active internet connection and an authorized OpenDojos account are required.
